A LITTLE ABOUT US

More than coffee.
A feeling.

Ember & Oak began with a simple idea: the best part of your day doesn’t have to be a big moment. Sometimes, it’s just a really good cup of coffee.

We’re a neighbourhood café for slow mornings, catch-ups that run a little long, and quiet afternoons with a book. Thoughtful coffee, honest food, and a seat that feels like yours.
